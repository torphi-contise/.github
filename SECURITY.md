# Security policy

This policy applies to every repository of the Torphi Contise organization that
does not publish its own.

Security issues must be reported privately so maintainers can investigate and
coordinate a fix before details enable exploitation.

## Supported versions

Until a repository publishes versioned releases, security fixes apply to its
current default branch. Historical snapshots and third-party forks are not
maintained by this organization.

## Report a vulnerability

Email `marco@torphi.com.br` with a subject that starts with `Security` and names
the affected repository.

Include only the information needed to investigate:

1. affected repository, component, route, type, or revision;
2. observed or potential impact;
3. minimal reproduction steps;
4. environment and relevant version information;
5. redacted evidence;
6. a safe contact method for follow-up.

Do not send valid credentials, passwords, one-time codes, reset tokens, session
tokens, provider secrets, or another person's data. If sensitive evidence is
essential, first describe what exists and arrange a safer transfer method.

## Expected response

The aim is to acknowledge a report within five business days and provide an
initial assessment within ten business days. These are communication goals, not
resolution guarantees.

## Responsible research

Use only systems, accounts, and data you own or are explicitly authorized to
test. Avoid denial of service, spam, social engineering, persistence, and access
to third-party data. Stop immediately if you encounter data you were not
authorized to access, and retain only the minimum evidence required.

This policy does not authorize testing third-party providers or infrastructure.
There is currently no bug bounty or guaranteed payment.

## Disclosure

Please do not publish exploitable details before a fix, mitigation, or agreed
disclosure date exists. Credit is offered when desired and safe.

Documentation errors, broken links, and non-security proposals may use public
issues.
